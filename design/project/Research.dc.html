Research

What does an AI answer cost in energy?

I measure how much energy AI models use, phase by phase. I'm also exploring whether a machine's power draw can show what an AI agent is doing on it.

JouleWise

Capstone research · advisor Prof. Suzanne Rivoire · June 2026 to present
What it is

JouleWise is an open-source, phase-aware instrument. It measures the energy of each phase of LLM inference on a laptop: reading the prompt, then writing the answer.

The readings come from the laptop's own power sensors and an inline USB-C meter. Each measurement session runs unattended for about six hours on a dedicated machine.

JouleWise on GitHub
Why it matters

I'm exploring power consumption as an out-of-band signal for catching rogue AI-agent behavior. Pilots are under way.

The idea comes from alignment research and recent AI-containment incidents. Power is measured outside the software an agent controls, which is what makes it interesting as a check.

How the results are protected

Custody controls keep the agents from tampering with or inventing results
1

Plans are sealed first

What is measured, which checks remove a session, and how the numbers will be analysed are fixed before any data is seen.

2

Results stay blind

Measured energies stay hidden until a recorded release, so no decision to repeat a session can depend on the result.

3

A second computation must agree

An independent recompute has to match before a result counts.
